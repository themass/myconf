kimi Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 13, 2026

Introduction

Welcome to kimi!

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share, and protect personal information when you use the kimi mobile application, related websites, and services (collectively, the "Services"). kimi is an AI chat assistant that lets you converse with an AI using text, voice messages, and images.

Data Controller. The Services are provided and controlled by the operator of kimi ("we", "us", or "our"). If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your personal data, please contact us at justbegin@gmail.com.

By using the Services, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Services.

What Information We Collect

We collect information in three ways: information you provide, information collected automatically, and information from other sources.

Information You Provide

  • Account information. If you create or use an account, we may collect your username, email address, phone number, password, or other registration details you choose to provide.
  • Chat and user content. When you use kimi, we collect the content you submit, including text messages, prompts, voice recordings, photos or images you upload, feedback, chat history, AI character settings, and other inputs ("Inputs"). We process these Inputs to generate AI responses ("Outputs").
  • Support and communications. If you contact us, we collect the information you send, such as your contact details, feedback, bug reports, or inquiries about your use of the Services.

Automatically Collected Information

  • Device and app information. We may collect your device model, operating system version, app version, language settings, device identifiers, IP address, and network connection type.
  • Usage and log information. We collect information about how you use the Services, such as features used, session duration, crash reports, performance logs, and diagnostic data.
  • Approximate location. We may infer approximate location from your IP address for security, fraud prevention, and service optimization.
  • Advertising identifiers. If you use the free version of kimi, we and our advertising partners may collect advertising IDs (such as Google Advertising ID) to deliver and measure ads, subject to your device settings and applicable law.
  • Cookies and similar technologies. If you access our websites or web-based features, we may use cookies and similar technologies for authentication, preferences, analytics, and security.

Information from Other Sources

  • Third-party sign-in. If you sign in through a third-party service (such as Google), we may receive information permitted by that service, such as your name, email address, or access token.
  • Service providers and security partners. We may receive information from partners that help us provide, secure, and improve the Services, such as fraud detection or analytics providers.

App Permissions

kimi may request the following device permissions to provide its features. You can manage many permissions in your device settings.

  • Internet — to connect to AI services, sync data, and load content.
  • Microphone — to record and send voice messages and to perform speech recognition (ASR).
  • Camera — to take photos and share images in chat.
  • Storage / Photos — to select, send, and save images in conversations.
  • Network state — to check connectivity and improve reliability.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide, operate, maintain, and improve the AI chat Services;
  • Process your Inputs and generate Outputs;
  • Enable voice messaging, image sharing, chat sessions, and account features;
  • Personalize your experience, including AI character settings;
  • Provide customer support and respond to your requests;
  • Send service-related notices, such as security alerts or policy updates;
  • Display advertisements in the free version of the app;
  • Monitor, detect, and prevent fraud, abuse, security incidents, and violations of our terms;
  • Analyze usage to improve performance, reliability, and user experience;
  • Train, evaluate, and improve our AI models and algorithms, where permitted by law and this Policy;
  • Comply with legal obligations and protect the rights, safety, and property of users and others.

How We Share Your Information

Service Providers

We share information with vendors that help us operate the Services, such as:

  • Cloud hosting and storage providers;
  • AI model and inference service providers that process your Inputs to generate Outputs;
  • Speech recognition (ASR) providers;
  • Analytics, crash reporting, and performance monitoring providers;
  • Advertising partners, including Google AdMob, to show and measure ads;
  • Customer support and communication tools.

These parties process personal data only as needed to perform services for us, under contractual obligations.

Other Disclosures

  • Legal requirements. We may disclose information if required by law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request, or to protect rights, safety, and security.
  • Business transfers. Information may be transferred in connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, or sale of assets.
  • With your consent. We may share information when you direct us to do so, such as when linking a third-party account or sharing content.

Third-party services linked from kimi (such as websites opened in-app) are governed by their own privacy policies. We are not responsible for third-party practices.

Data Retention

We retain personal information only as long as necessary to provide the Services, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. Chat history and account data may be retained until you delete them or your account, unless a longer retention period is required by law.

Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to certain processing of your personal information, or to withdraw consent where processing is consent-based.

  • You may delete chat history or your account through in-app settings, where available;
  • You may opt out of personalized ads via your device advertising settings (e.g., "Opt out of Ads Personalization" on Android);
  • You may contact us at justbegin@gmail.com to exercise your privacy rights.

We will respond to verified requests in accordance with applicable law.

AI Output Accuracy

kimi generates responses by predicting text based on your Inputs. Outputs may be inaccurate, incomplete, or outdated. You should not rely on Outputs as professional, medical, legal, financial, or other expert advice. If you believe Outputs contain inaccurate information about you, contact us to request correction or deletion where applicable.

Children's Privacy

The Services are not directed to children under 14 (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

International Data Transfers

Your information may be processed and stored in countries other than your own, including countries that may have different data protection laws. Where required, we implement appropriate safeguards for cross-border transfers.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated policy and revise the "Last updated" date. Continued use of the Services after changes become effective constitutes acceptance of the updated Policy.
